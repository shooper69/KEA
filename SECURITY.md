# Security

Kea is a live product at [https://kea.chat](https://kea.chat). We take the safety of accounts, conversations, and payments seriously.

## Report a vulnerability

Email **[team@kea.chat](mailto:team@kea.chat)** with:

- A short description of the issue
- The affected URL, app screen, or API path on kea.chat
- Steps to reproduce, if you have them
- Impact (for example: other users’ data, account takeover, payment)

Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security reports, and do not share exploit details outside that email until we have had a chance to respond.

## What to expect

We read reports at team@kea.chat. We aim to acknowledge within a few working days and to keep you informed while we investigate.

## Scope

In scope: [kea.chat](https://kea.chat), the Kea Android app that talks to that site, and the services that site uses for sign-in, talk, and billing.

Out of scope: social-engineering of people, physical access, and reports that depend on a compromised device or leaked third-party credentials.

## Safe harbour

If you report in good faith, stay within the scope above, avoid privacy harm and service disruption, and give us time to fix the issue before public disclosure, we will not pursue legal action for that research.

## Data and accounts

To delete your own Kea account and data, use [https://kea.chat/delete-account](https://kea.chat/delete-account) or Settings → Security in the app. Privacy: [https://kea.chat/privacy-policy](https://kea.chat/privacy-policy).
