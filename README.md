# Kea

**Elevator pitch:** Kea is a language learning chatty companion. You acquire a language by talking with her—the way you acquired your first language—through meaning, memory, and real conversation at [kea.chat](https://kea.chat).

## What is Kea?

Kea is a language learning chatty companion, live at [https://kea.chat](https://kea.chat). People pick up a language by talking with her like a friend, not by taking a course, sitting with a tutor, or drilling grammar tables. You speak one language and talk with Kea in another: English, Spanish, French, German, or Russian. She remembers what you have spoken about, notices words and phrases you struggle with, and keeps them on a personal Learn List until you use them well. There is no lesson plan, streak, or classroom. Open [kea.chat](https://kea.chat) to start a conversation.

## Overview

Kea’s mission is language *acquisition through companionship*. Humans did not learn to speak from verb tables. They learned by listening, repeating, feeling meaning, and wanting to be understood. Kea is built for that process.

At [kea.chat](https://kea.chat), Kea talks with you in the language you are acquiring. She meets you at your level, keeps the thread of everyday life—meals, work, curiosity, stories—and uses the language you need until it belongs to you. Progress is the growing ease of conversation, not a badge.

Kea is **not** a learning platform, tutor, chatbot drill, or game. She is a companion. Read how that works on [The Method](https://kea.chat/method) and the short definition on [What is Kea](https://kea.chat/what-is-kea).

## Key Features

- **Hands-free talk** — Speak with Kea and hear her reply. Conversation is the product, not a side mode. Start at [kea.chat](https://kea.chat).
- **Companion, not curriculum** — Unstructured talk about real life. No forced path back to “coffee or greetings.”
- **Learn List** — Words and phrases you struggle with are kept until you use them well, then they leave.
- **Remembered topics** — Threads you have spoken about can be picked up again, the way friendship works.
- **Gentle correction in conversation** — Mistakes are noticed in flow, not as a test.
- **Languages you choose** — You speak one of English, Spanish, French, German, or Russian, and talk with Kea in another.
- **Voices and personality** — Choose how Kea sounds. Higher subscription tiers add more sophisticated talk and character.
- **Background performance** — Talk time and trend are tracked quietly. No stars, pings, or ads pushing you to perform.
- **Web and Android** — Use Kea in the browser at [kea.chat](https://kea.chat) and as an Android app.
- **Account and privacy** — Sign in, manage subscription, and delete your data when you choose ([Delete my Kea data](https://kea.chat/delete-account), [Privacy](https://kea.chat/privacy-policy)).

## Who Kea Is For

Kea is for people who want to *speak*, not study at a screen.

- Adults acquiring a second language after work, in the car, or while moving through the day
- Travellers and residents who need real conversation, not a syllabus
- Professionals who already “know grammar” but freeze when they have to talk
- Families and partners who want a patient companion, not a teacher persona
- Anyone tired of gamified courses, streaks, and being sent back to lesson one

Industries and contexts: consumer language acquisition, travel and relocation, everyday bilingual life, and personal practice—not schools, LMS platforms, or corporate training suites.

## Use Cases

- **Commute talk** — Hands-free conversation in Spanish (or French, German, Russian, English) while driving or walking.
- **Confidence after courses** — You finished an app or a class; Kea is where speech becomes usable.
- **Living abroad** — Keep talking about shops, neighbours, work, and weather so the language does not rust.
- **Returning to a language** — Pick up topics you already care about instead of restarting a tree of units.
- **Practice without performance** — No leaderboard. You talk; Kea answers; the Learn List holds the gaps.
- **Pair with books, not replace life** — Use grammar books if you wish; use [kea.chat](https://kea.chat) so knowledge does not sit unused.

## Why Kea?

Most language products are courses with a microphone bolted on. Kea is the opposite: conversation first, structure in the background.

She is named for the kea—the curious alpine parrot of New Zealand—intelligent, social, and always learning by exploring. That is the product: a companion who talks with you, remembers you, and grows with the relationship. [The Method](https://kea.chat/method) is the public statement of that difference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Kea?**  
Kea is a language learning chatty companion. She talks with you like a friend so you acquire language through conversation. Details: [https://kea.chat/what-is-kea](https://kea.chat/what-is-kea).

**Is Kea a language learning app or tutor?**  
No. Kea is not a course, tutor, classroom, or grammar drill. She is a companion. Understanding comes first; speech follows.

**How do I start?**  
Open [https://kea.chat](https://kea.chat), create a free account, and talk. Read [The Method](https://kea.chat/method) if you want the idea before you speak.

**Which languages does Kea support?**  
English, Spanish, French, German, and Russian. You speak one and talk with Kea in another.

**What is the Learn List?**  
It holds words and phrases Kea noticed you struggling with. Items leave when you use them well in conversation.

**Does Kea remember what we talked about?**  
Yes. Topics you have spoken about can be continued later, like any ongoing relationship.

**Is there gamification?**  
No stars, streaks, or ad interruptions. Progress is your growing ability to chat. Performance is tracked in the background if you want to see talk time.

**Is Kea free?**  
You can start from [kea.chat](https://kea.chat). Talk time is offered on a trial, then subscription. Tiers add sophistication of conversation and character.

**Where do I get help?**  
Email [team@kea.chat](mailto:team@kea.chat) or use [Contact](https://kea.chat/contact) and [Customer Support](https://kea.chat/support).

**How do I delete my data?**  
Use [Delete my Kea data](https://kea.chat/delete-account) or Settings → Security in the app. See also the [Privacy Policy](https://kea.chat/privacy-policy).

**Is there an Android app?**  
Yes. Kea is available on the web at [kea.chat](https://kea.chat) and as an Android application.

**Can I use Kea without a lesson plan?**  
Yes. That is the point. You talk about life. Kea answers. The Learn List holds the gaps.

## Technology

Kea is a production web and Android product.

- **Client:** React, TypeScript, Vite, progressive web app
- **Android:** Capacitor wrapping the same web application
- **Backend and data:** Supabase (Kea Production) for accounts and cloud data
- **Hosting:** Netlify, site `keachat`, domain [kea.chat](https://kea.chat)
- **Conversation:** speech in, speech out, and an AI companion that talks at your level
- **Payments:** subscription billing for talk time and higher-sophistication tiers
- **Public site:** [What is Kea](https://kea.chat/what-is-kea), [The Method](https://kea.chat/method), legal pages, [llms.txt](https://kea.chat/llms.txt), and [sitemap.xml](https://kea.chat/sitemap.xml) for search and AI crawlers
